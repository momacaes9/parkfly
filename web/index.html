Data sources & assumptions
Cached official geometry and capacity. Spot positions estimated.

Occupancy is simulated with fixed seeds. Accuracy is measured only on unsensed spots; observations are excluded from that score.

Model: spots fill roughly in order of attractiveness, so a few sensed spots pin down one fill level per lot. Naive baseline: every unsensed spot gets the occupied share of the sensed ones.

Nearby lots share demand. Put sensors on one whole lot and its neighbors' estimates move too, with uncertainty that grows with distance.
