# Assumptions and data provenance

These are the current defaults; they supersede earlier session numbers.

- **Official NCSU**: cached GIS snapshot, 214 source features, 203 active/existing positive-capacity polygons displayed. Rechecked source returned HTTP 200 and matching focus counts. The API contains geometry, capacity and permit counts; it supplies **no live spot occupancy**.
- **Focus**: preserve seven modeled permit sections (478 spots), six physical facilities. Poulton's visitor and C-permit sections share one deck. Deck grids are schematic; no verified floor allocation is claimed.
- **Destination**: Hunt Library by default. Hunt and Riddick pins are cached OSM/Nominatim results with source IDs in `data/destinations.json`. Riddick updates walking costs; simulator demand and evaluation remain for Hunt. No claim of validating main-campus parking occupancy.
- **Stalls**: estimated using the sibling Park Shark grid method: 2.6 × 5.4 m bays, 6.2 m aisles, setback and orientation/grid search, trimmed to official counts. Where the footprint cannot fit the official count, a schematic lattice is explicitly labeled. Existing local metric projection retained; this is not a surveyed UTM/building-exclusion plan.
- **Entrances**: boundary point nearest destination, because Overpass road lookup timed out. Walking = 1.35 × straight line / 80 m per minute. Pay rate $2/hour is an assumption; simplified commuter/visitor eligibility does not establish legal parking permission. Weekend restrictions require sign verification.
- **Ground truth**: seeded, minute-step driver simulation, five-minute snapshots. Training weekdays 100–113; training Saturdays 200–205. Held-out weekdays 1–4 and Saturday 50. Commuter and visitor demand, anchor weights, dwell times and 30% Saturday multiplier are assumptions in `pipeline/simulate_drivers.py`.
- **Posterior**: 101-point fill grid. Morning and afternoon frontier softness are fit separately on training weekdays; priors on training weekdays/Saturdays. NC State covariance is fit on simulated training residuals, not measured parking behavior. No promise of gains in every distance bin.
- **Sensors**: idealized 12-neighbor clusters, about $65 each, matching the stated 10–15-spot range. Individual scattered spots are a nonphysical reference. Hardware count rounds up within each section. Partial clusters represent the requested sensed budget; real installation geometry remains unverified.
- **Within-lot result**: at 25%, held-out unsensed-spot Brier 0.1232 vs naive 0.1789; accuracy 81.9% vs 72.9%. At 100% there are no unsensed targets, so the corresponding score is n/a.
- **Birmingham real-data result**: 28 retained lots, chronological 51/22 train/test days. UK local timestamps rounded to half hours; broken rows dropped; counts divided by capacity. Coverage selection and hour × weekday mean use training only. No interpolation. Pairwise empirical residual covariance is projected to PSD with a 2% ridge. Same hidden lots and available timestamps for both policies, 200 draws, seed 7. At 25%, MAE 0.0702 → 0.0473, a 32.5% reduction. At 100%, hidden-target MAE is null.
- **Fly**: fixed FlyWire/Shiu v783 signed connectome, 138,639 neurons; supplied complete surface contains 480 measured runs, three seeds per condition. It is a fixed spiking filter, not 139k neurons trained on parking. Sanity table was rerun locally and all six passed; full response surface/raster are preserved from the earlier run.
- **Encoding**: probability of an available spot is transformed from expected free count (scale 3), discounted by walk (8-minute scale). Bitter uses normalized posterior uncertainty, permit risk and price. Six-percentage-point predicted fill rise over 15 min maps to the full 50 Hz looming range. These are engineering mappings, not biologically learned parking semantics. Senses clamp to measured surface bounds; no unsupported extrapolation.
- **Verdicts**: Giant Fibre ≥130 Hz diverts (roughly half the looming sanity response); otherwise MDN > MN9 or MN9 <5 Hz backs off; otherwise commit. The Giant Fibre lesion zeros input columns (`W @ diag(keep)`), not output rows. Other circuits can still back off.
- **Measured fly comparison**: at 25%, fly 1.76 min, 96.2% success; nearest-lot 1.60 min, 96.2% success. Earlier 1.40-minute/tie numbers are superseded after geocoding and looming calibration. Score is walking plus a five-minute full-lot penalty; no live traffic or route-time optimization is claimed.
- **Lesion scene**: searched held-out simulation states, shuffle 1, day 0, step 46, 25% sensed, E.B. III / Entrepreneur Drive. 5 simulated free now, 0 after 15 minutes. Intact DIVERT → input-lesioned COMMIT is measured by surface interpolation. This selected scene demonstrates circuit behavior; it is not an unbiased policy-performance estimate.
- **Raster**: three recorded canonical brain runs, labeled as playback; browser verdicts use trilinear response-surface interpolation, not live full-brain simulation.
- **Offline**: all scripts, fonts, tables and data are local. Basemap fallback is OSM → Carto → plain polygons/spots. Optional Google Maps requires a referrer-restricted browser key and has an auth-failure fallback; no key is bundled. Font licenses are preserved.
- **Account access**: no key, FlyWire token, OAuth credential, anonymous deployment claim token or Vercel credential is committed. Vercel production is live at https://parkfly-one.vercel.app in monica-a634/parkfly. GitHub writes are still rejected by the connected integration; local commits and a bundle preserve work.

- **Live location**: explicit browser permission is requested only by the Use my location button. `watchPosition` updates a blue marker/accuracy circle and sugar's trip-cost input. Coordinates are kept only in page memory and cleared on Stop/page exit; Parkfly does not upload them. Basemap providers see tile requests for the viewed area. Driving estimate = straight-line distance ×1.3 / 20 km/h, added to destination walking time. No traffic feed or actual road routing is claimed. Scripted demo clears GPS to preserve reproducibility.
- **Network graphic**: group-to-group synapse weights are computed from the real signed connectivity file by `pipeline/export_fly_network.py`. The drawing collapses other neurons and shows selected projections; it is not an individual-neuron rendering. Width uses total signed-file synapse magnitude; green/red denotes net excitation/inhibition. GF input projection is dashed under lesion.
- Follow-up connection UI: connecting GPS automatically opens Fly brain and checks official NCSU geometry/capacity. The card distinguishes GPS, cached vs verified official data, and real measured neural-table output. Queued location callbacks are ignored after Stop; no GPS coordinate is uploaded to the NCSU endpoint.

## Fly-focused simulation lab and encoder calibration (current)
- Latest user direction: app identity is 🪰 ParkFly. Removed the previous startup branding, device pricing, and commercial installation copy from the interface. Historical code attribution remains in repository credits.
- The uploaded NC State starter PDF confirms NumPy/pandas/SciPy sparse for full-brain simulation and FAFBseg 3.2.3/Navis 1.12.0/Plotly/crackle-codec for optional skeleton visualization. The PDF shows a missing CAVE secret in that optional visualization; public-table brain simulations require no token.
- Optional visual dependencies are separate in requirements-visualization.txt. They were not installed or exercised with a private token in this task. The downloadable Colab notebook keeps this visualization disabled by default.
- Calibrated only the parking-to-sense encoder; FlyWire synapses and neuronal thresholds remain unchanged. Grid: 81 combinations of walk scale, uncertainty gain, fill-rise gain, and near-full gain. Chosen on weekdays 100–113, over 0/10/25/50% observation coverage, by maximizing free-spot success then minimizing walking plus five-minute failure penalty.
- Selected values: walk scale 2 min, uncertainty gain 0.5, fill-rise gain 0.5, near-full gain 1. The calibrated sugar averages availability over the fill posterior before discounting by travel cost.
- Evaluation: weekdays 1–4, 156 decision cases per density, same masks for all policies (seed 5000 + coverage). At 25%: calibrated 151/156 = 96.79%, original 150/156 = 96.15%; mean costs 1.583 and 1.763 min. Nearest-lot is 150/156 at 1.599 min. Oracle is 154/156 at 1.471 min.
- Gain is one case: 0.641 percentage points; 95% paired interval 0–1.923 pp from 2000 whole-day resamples, seed 20261114. This includes zero and does not establish a real-world improvement. Calibrated intact and calibrated lesioned policies tie at 25% aggregate success. The selected original-encoder lesion scene is a separate circuit demonstration.
- Dashboard accuracy now excludes observed spots. Whole-map accuracy remains an explicitly named secondary chart option. Training performance, test performance, single-snapshot inference and a selected lesion example are kept distinct.
- Decision quality is for a C-permit commuter to Hunt Library and the selected lot's current simulated snapshot. The score includes a fixed full-lot penalty and walking; GPS drive estimates are excluded from the benchmark. Visitor/GPS cases are exploratory.
